I'm a Master's student in Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by [Prof. William Cohen](https://wwcohen.github.io/). My research looks at how LLM agents can improve from their own experience without any parameter updates. My first-author paper, [Learning Reusable Agent Subroutines from Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02994) (ARISE, under review at ICLR 2027), mines an agent's successful traces into a library of natural-language subroutines, each executed by its own LLM call. A companion paper, [Learning to Construct Practical Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00189), studies how to build modular agent systems automatically rather than by hand. I also worked on [SpecDrop](/publication/2026-specdrop) (NeurIPS 2026), a parameter-free routing method for modular specialization.

Before CMU I worked with [Prof. Liang Zhao](https://cs.emory.edu/~lzhao41/) at the Emory Machine Learning Lab on retrieval-augmented generation over graph-structured data. That work produced [GRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16506) (Findings of NAACL 2025) and [CG-RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15067) (SIGIR 2025). I received my B.S. in Physics with a minor in Computer Science from Emory University in 2023.

In summer 2026 I was a software engineer intern at Amazon Ads on the Full-Funnel Agentic Intelligence and Models (FAIM) team, where I built an agentic analytics compiler that replaces manual Redshift-to-PartiQL migration: an LLM agent transpiles queries, validates them by executing against benchmark cases, and auto-generates analytics dashboards.

Research interests
======
- **LLM reasoning & agents** — self-improving agents, learning reusable subroutines from experience, tool use
- **Retrieval-augmented generation** — graph-structured retrieval, citation-aware retrieval, hybrid sparse/dense methods
- **Modular networks** — mixture-of-experts, category-conditioned specialization, domain routing

Outside of research I do independent quantitative research — text-based equity signals, options volatility, and statistical arbitrage.
